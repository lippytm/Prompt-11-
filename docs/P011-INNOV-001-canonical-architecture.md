## Purpose

P-011-INNOV-001 converts registered Prompt #11 downloads, CRM signals, learner needs, customer problems, environmental findings, technology opportunities, and creative concepts into bounded innovation portfolios, experiments, prototypes, learning products, and ethical revenue hypotheses.

The Forge does not equate idea volume with innovation. An innovation must be connected to a problem, evidence, user or ecosystem value, a test, risks, correction, and a human decision.

## Innovation lifecycle

`signal → problem definition → artifact/source intake → concept passport → independent model-line treatments → score → sandbox experiment → prototype → test and red team → learning/product adaptation → business experiment → human decision → release, revise, quarantine, or retire`
