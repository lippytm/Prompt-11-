# P-011-DSF-001 Source Register

**Version:** 0.1  
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-22  
**Scope:** DARPA, Total Information Awareness, Snowden-era surveillance disclosures, FISA oversight, identity protection, medical-record integrity, UAP disclosure, information warfare, and publication governance.

## Source policy

Use this order of preference:

1. statutes, court opinions, official records, declassified documents, inspectors general, and oversight bodies
2. peer-reviewed research and recognized technical standards
3. original reporting from established news organizations
4. identified witness testimony and advocacy materials
5. unattributed claims, social-media posts, and speculative interpretations

A lower-tier source may identify a lead, but it cannot independently establish an extraordinary factual claim. Every source record should include access date, author or institution, publication date, title, URL, claim supported, limitations, and archive location.

## A. DARPA and Total Information Awareness

### S-001 — DARPA, About DARPA

- **Type:** Official agency mission page
- **URL:** https://www.darpa.mil/about
- **Supports:** DARPA's research-and-development mission; transformational and dual-use technology role; institutional distinction from intelligence and law-enforcement agencies.
- **Limitations:** Agency self-description; does not independently evaluate civil-liberties effects.

### S-002 — Consolidated Appropriations Resolution, 2003, H.J.Res. 2 / Public Law 108-7

- **Type:** Statute and CRS legislative summary
- **URL:** https://www.congress.gov/bill/108th-congress/house-joint-resolution/2
- **Supports:** Congress imposed reporting, authorization, and privacy-related restrictions on research and deployment connected to Total Information Awareness.
- **Limitations:** Read the enacted text and statutory notes, not only a summary, when making a precise legal claim.

### S-003 — Department of Defense Appropriations Act, 2004 conference report, Section 8131

- **Type:** Congressional record
- **URL:** https://www.congress.gov/committee-report/108th-congress/house-report/283/1
- **Supports:** Funding prohibition directed at Terrorism Information Awareness / Total Information Awareness and defined exceptions for certain lawful overseas military and foreign-intelligence activities.
- **Limitations:** Historical appropriations language; not evidence that every later data-analysis program was a continuation of TIA.

### S-004 — 10 U.S.C. § 2241 statutory notes: Total Information Awareness Program

- **Type:** Codified statutory notes
- **URL:** https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10/2241
- **Supports:** Contemporaneous limitations, reporting requirements, deployment controls, and congressional civil-liberties concerns.
- **Limitations:** Cornell LII is a reliable legal-access service, but official U.S. Code and Statutes at Large should be retained in the archive for formal legal work.

## B. Snowden, criminal status, and public disclosures

### S-010 — Department of Justice statement on Snowden provisional-arrest request

- **Type:** Official prosecutorial statement
- **URL:** https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/justice-department-statement-request-hong-kong-edward-snowden-s-provisional-arrest
- **Supports:** The 2013 federal charges alleged unauthorized disclosure of national-defense information, unauthorized disclosure of classified communications intelligence, and theft of government property.
- **Required wording:** Charges are allegations unless adjudicated. Do not describe Snowden as convicted on these criminal counts.

### S-011 — United States obtains final civil judgment concerning prepublication review

- **Type:** Official DOJ statement concerning civil litigation
- **URL:** https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/united-states-obtains-final-judgment-and-permanent-injunction-against-edward-snowden
- **Supports:** A separate civil judgment found breach of CIA and NSA prepublication-review obligations concerning the memoir *Permanent Record* and specified speeches.
- **Limitations:** This civil judgment is not a judgment on the merits of every surveillance disclosure and is separate from the unresolved criminal case.

### S-012 — National Security Archive and other curated public document collections

- **Type:** Archival research collection
- **URL:** https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/
- **Supports:** Discovery of declassified and publicly released government records and historical context.
- **Limitations:** Verify every document's provenance and use the original agency or court copy when available.

## C. Section 215 bulk telephone records

### S-020 — PCLOB Report on the Telephone Records Program Conducted Under Section 215

- **Type:** Independent executive-branch oversight report
- **URL:** https://www.pclob.gov/Oversight
- **Supports:** Program description; PCLOB's conclusion that the bulk telephone-records program lacked a viable legal foundation under Section 215, raised constitutional and policy concerns, and demonstrated limited value; oversight recommendations.
- **Limitations:** Report contains legal and policy judgments; distinguish the Board's conclusions from final judicial holdings.

### S-021 — USA FREEDOM Act of 2015

- **Type:** Federal statute
- **URL:** https://www.congress.gov/bill/114th-congress/house-bill/2048
- **Supports:** Legislative termination of the earlier government bulk telephone-metadata framework and creation of a more targeted call-detail-record mechanism.
- **Limitations:** Later operational changes and expirations must be researched separately.

### S-022 — United States v. Moalin, Ninth Circuit opinion, 2020

- **Type:** Federal appellate opinion
- **URL:** https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2020/09/02/13-50572.pdf
- **Supports:** The Ninth Circuit concluded that the telephony metadata collection at issue exceeded statutory authorization and may have violated the Fourth Amendment, while affirming the convictions because the metadata did not affect the outcome.
- **Limitations:** State the court's actual holding precisely; do not generalize it to every intelligence program.

## D. Section 702, PRISM, upstream collection, and oversight

### S-030 — PCLOB Report on the Surveillance Program Operated Pursuant to Section 702

- **Type:** Oversight report
- **URL:** https://www.pclob.gov/Oversight
- **Supports:** Public description of Section 702, PRISM, upstream collection, targeting, minimization, legal analysis, benefits, risks, and recommendations.
- **Limitations:** Historical report; later statutes, FISC opinions, procedures, and compliance reports may supersede details.

### S-031 — ODNI release of FISC Section 702 certification opinion and procedures

- **Type:** Declassified official records
- **URL:** https://www.odni.gov/3555/pr-29-25/
- **Supports:** Public release of a FISC opinion and associated targeting, minimization, and querying procedures; official description of compliance reforms and legal review.
- **Limitations:** Redactions remain; release does not answer every policy or constitutional dispute.

### S-032 — ODNI Annual Intelligence Community Statistical Transparency Report

- **Type:** Official annual statistics
- **URL:** https://www.odni.gov/12842/pr-05-26/
- **Supports:** Date-specific statistics and explanations for intelligence authorities, including Section 702 targets and U.S.-person query terms.
- **Limitations:** Counting methods and definitions must accompany numerical comparisons.

### S-033 — Reforming Intelligence and Securing America Act, Public Law 118-49

- **Type:** Federal statute
- **URL:** https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/7888
- **Supports:** 2024 reauthorization and reforms to FISA Title VII and Section 702.
- **Limitations:** The statute's sunset and later temporary extensions require current-law verification.

### S-034 — Current Section 702 status tracker

- **Type:** Date-sensitive legal and policy analysis
- **URL:** https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/section-702-foreign-intelligence-surveillance-act
- **Supports:** Analysis of the 2026 statutory lapse, transition rules, and reported continuation of existing court-approved certifications.
- **Required control:** Verify against the latest Congress, ODNI, DOJ, FISC, and reputable contemporaneous reporting before every release. Never reuse the 2026 status paragraph without a fresh check.

## H. Current conflicts and information warfare

Current Ukraine, Iran, cyberwarfare, sanctions, casualty, territorial-control, and officeholder claims must be researched anew for each release. Store the publication date and event date separately. Prefer official statements for what an institution claims, but corroborate battlefield and casualty claims with independent reporting and recognized monitoring organizations.

No static source register can make a current-war chapter evergreen.

## I. Copyright, NFT, and classified-information controls

- Summarize and cite public records; do not reproduce copyrighted books, documentaries, journalism, or extensive document collections without permission.
- Do not mint, sell, or store private records, leaked personal data, unpublished classified material, or confidential evidence.
- Use original artwork and original narrative transformations.
- A content hash proves file integrity and timestamped existence; it does not prove the truth of the file's claims.
- NFT ownership and copyright ownership are separate unless the governing license expressly connects them.
