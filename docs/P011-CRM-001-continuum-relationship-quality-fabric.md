## 1. Purpose

P-011-CRM-001 makes relationship management a first-class Prompt #11 operating system rather than a separate sales list. It governs how the Business of Businesses understands, serves, teaches, supports, corrects, and learns from relationships with:

- learners and customers;
- creators and contributors;
- affiliates, mentors, partners, and franchise candidates;
- members, supporters, communities, and organizations;
- internal human operators and disclosed AI interfaces.

CRM means **Continuum Relationship Management, Customer Success, and Quality Fabric**. It is provider-neutral. A CRM vendor, spreadsheet, database, email service, form, Slack channel, chatbot, or website may be an adapter, but none of them silently becomes the canonical meaning of the relationship.

## 2. Core relationship principle

A relationship is not merely a contact record. It is a governed lifecycle containing:

1. identity and provenance;
2. role and relationship context;
3. contact preferences and verified channels;
4. purpose, consent, lawful basis, suppression, and retention;
5. interactions and journey state;
6. learning, customer-success, support, and community outcomes;
7. opportunities, offers, memberships, referrals, and partnerships;
8. attribution, revenue reference, costs, refunds, complaints, and corrections;
9. quality findings, risks, gates, approvals, audits, and retirement.

No record may be used for a purpose that is materially different from the approved purpose without a new review.

## 3. Environmental Ecosystem integration

### Human environment

Track dignity, accessibility, communication preferences, attention, workload, support needs, consent, and the right to stop or correct processing.

### Natural environment

Track aggregate product, campaign, event, delivery, device, and lifecycle impacts. Avoid inferring environmental characteristics about a person unless necessary, lawful, transparent, and reviewed.

### Social-political environment

Protect rights, fairness, non-discrimination, community trust, whistleblower safety, due process, appeal, and correction. CRM data must not become a covert political-targeting or social-scoring system.

### Knowledge environment

Record learning goals, interests, source provenance, uncertainty, feedback, correction history, and demonstrated outcomes. A model-generated guess is not a verified preference.

### Creative environment

Connect characters, stories, media, Educational Entertainment experiences, and creative universes while keeping fictional identity separate from legal identity.

### Digital-cyber environment

Govern authentication, contact verification, privacy classification, security, connector permissions, event correlation, diagnostics, incident response, backup, restore, revocation, and auditability.

### Learning environment

Connect pathways, skills, lessons, assessments, mentors, projects, accommodations, evidence of learning, learner support, and Learning-to-Earning contributions.

### Business-economic environment

Connect lead source, customer problem, journey, offer, product, campaign, support, opportunity, attribution, accounting reference, costs, retention, satisfaction, and correction.

### Cosmic-speculative environment

Fictional characters, clones, Time Machines, and intergalactic roles remain marked as fiction. A fictional persona cannot silently become the real-world identity of a customer, learner, creator, or operator.

Many-to-many relationships use explicit link records. Comma-separated IDs are not canonical database relationships.

## 5. Identity resolution

Identity resolution is a suggestion-and-review process, not an automatic merge.

Required controls:

- source and provenance for every identity attribute;
- normalization without destroying original evidence;
- confidence and matching reason;
- duplicate-candidate record;
- human adjudication for merges and splits;
- reversible merge history;
- no merging solely because names are similar;
- no combining personal, business, fictional, clone, or public identities without explicit authorization;
- correction propagation to every affected system.

## 6. Consent, purpose, and communication

Before outreach or a personalized action, the system must establish:

- declared purpose;
- approved channel;
- consent or another documented lawful basis when applicable;
- suppression and do-not-contact status;
- contact verification state;
- privacy class;
- accessibility and communication preferences;
- retention and review date;
- identity of the human or system proposing the action;
- HumanApprovalGate when required.

An AI may explain choices, draft a communication, or propose a mission. It cannot grant consent on behalf of a person, revoke a person's rights, silently change the purpose, or override suppression.

## 7. AI Clone and Fabric Hermes integration

Disclosed AI Clone interfaces may:

- organize approved relationship records;
- draft bounded communications;
- propose learning or support missions;
- detect incomplete data, consent gaps, duplicate candidates, overdue support, or correction needs;
- summarize trends using minimum-necessary data;
- generate quality evidence and Continuation Packets.

They may not:

- impersonate Charles Earl Lipshay or another person;
- fabricate a personal memory, preference, relationship, promise, or testimonial;
- silently merge identities;
- create hidden sensitive profiles;
- change consent or suppression;
- make high-impact eligibility decisions;
- promise income, employment, funding, health, learning, or business outcomes;
- approve refunds, payments, contracts, credit, investments, or asset transfers;
- pass HumanApprovalGate;
- broaden their own permissions or activate their own mutation.

Fabric Hermes routes CRM events as bounded work packets. Every packet includes purpose, party or aggregate scope, privacy class, model line, source reference, permitted tools, destination, budget, timeout, required gates, audit correlation, rollback, revocation, and correction route.

## 8. CRM lifecycle

`capture → validate → classify → consent review → identity review → journey placement → bounded interaction → learning/service delivery → outcome measurement → correction → retention review → renewal, suppression, retirement, or archive`

Additional states include `blocked`, `quarantined`, `revoked`, and `disputed`.

Every transition records actor, timestamp, evidence, reason summary, correlation ID, and applicable gate state.

## 9. Customer and learner journeys

Journey types may include:

- learner;
- customer;
- creator or contributor;
- affiliate;
- mentor;
- partner;
- member;
- supporter or community participant;
- franchise candidate or node.

Typical stages include discovery, consent, qualified, onboarding, activated, learning, delivery, support, retention, renewal, advocacy, dormancy, correction, and retirement.

Journey movement must be evidence-based and reversible. A journey stage is not a moral score and must not be used as hidden eligibility logic.

## 10. Customer success, learner success, and support

CRM success measures more than conversion. It includes:

- learner progress and demonstrated skills;
- accessibility barriers discovered and corrected;
- support responsiveness and resolution;
- customer value and satisfaction;
- complaint, refund, and correction patterns;
- retention without coercion;
- mentor, partner, and affiliate quality;
- contribution and community outcomes;
- privacy, fairness, and security outcomes;
- environmental and operational effects.

Support cases record severity, owner, response and resolution targets, privacy class, correction route, and human-only refund authority.

## 11. Opportunity, attribution, and revenue integrity

CRM connects lead source, campaign, workflow, agent, platform, product, opportunity, and revenue reference. It does not replace accounting records.

Rules:

- estimated attribution is marked estimated;
- confirmed attribution requires an accounting reference;
- missing attribution is an operational issue, not automatic accounting fraud;
- an AI cannot set price, contract, payment, refund, credit, tax, investment, or asset authority by itself;
- forecasts and lead scores must disclose assumptions and uncertainty;
- no guaranteed earnings, funding, employment, or investment return claims;
- affiliate and referral relationships require disclosure.

## 17. Stop-work conditions

Stop or quarantine work when:

- purpose or consent is absent;
- suppression is ignored;
- identity confidence is inadequate;
- a hidden sensitive profile is created;
- restricted data is routed to an unauthorized platform;
- an AI attempts a merge, refund, contract, payment, credit, investment, tax, medical, legal, employment, housing, insurance, or political decision;
- fairness, accessibility, privacy, security, or financial-authority controls fail;
- an attribution claim lacks required evidence;
- a correction is not propagated;
- a connector lacks shutdown or revocation;
- costs exceed the approved limit;
- HumanApprovalGate is bypassed.
