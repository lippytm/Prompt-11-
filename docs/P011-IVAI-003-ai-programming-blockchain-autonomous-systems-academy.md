# P-011-IVAI-003 — AI Programming, Blockchain, and Autonomous Systems Academy

## 1. Mission

Make the Grand Master AI Engine and Prompt #11 Interactive Video AI Platform a **dual training system**:

1. train people to become better programmers, blockchain developers, AI builders, system designers, and technical entrepreneurs; and
2. train governed AI agents and clone systems to become better at planning, coding, testing, documenting, evaluating, and maintaining software and blockchain systems.

The Academy is not a passive course library. It is a **learning-by-building environment** where instruction, simulation, coding, GitHub workflows, AI evaluation, interactive video, business application, and measurable skill growth are connected.

## 2. Core principle

Every important concept should be teachable in at least four forms:

- **Explain Mode** — clear conceptual instruction;
- **Demonstrate Mode** — worked interactive examples;
- **Build Mode** — hands-on implementation;
- **Prove Mode** — tests, evidence, review, and learner explanation.

Where appropriate, a fifth form is added:

- **Business Mode** — convert the technical skill into a real customer/business use case without guaranteed-income claims.

## 3. Learner types

The system should support:

## 9. Human + AI pair-programming world

The Interactive Video AI Platform should support a persistent coding world where the learner can:

1. ask an AI mentor a question;
2. watch a concept demonstration;
3. enter a coding workspace;
4. make or approve changes;
5. run tests;
6. see failures explained visually;
7. ask another AI specialist for a second opinion;
8. compare alternative implementations;
9. commit approved work to GitHub;
10. explain what was learned;
11. earn a Skill Evidence record based on actual demonstrated work.

## 10. AI training and evaluation loop

AI agents should also be trained through controlled evaluation.

Cycle:

`task → agent attempt → tests → evidence review → red-team critique → compare against rubric → identify capability gap → revised instructions/tools/workflow → rerun → regression test → promotion or rejection`

The system does not represent this as autonomous model-weight training unless an actual supported training pipeline is used. In most cases, improvement will mean better prompts, tools, RAG, workflows, examples, policies, routing, and evaluations.

Examples:

- validate inputs;

- add tests;
- document the project;

### Quest: Build a Smart Contract Safely
- understand the contract objective;
- write a local/testnet-only contract;
- test expected behavior;
- test failure cases;

## 19. Safety and governance

The Academy and Builder must not:

- teach credential theft, malware deployment, unauthorized intrusion, or evasion;
- request production seed phrases/private keys;
- auto-deploy financial transactions without approved controls;
- misrepresent AI-generated accomplishments as unassisted human work;
- issue false professional credentials;
- promise guaranteed business or investment returns;
- bypass Prompt #11 rights, evidence, privacy, security, and human-approval gates.
