# P-011-EESI-001 — Fable 5 and Ethical Revenue Swarm Manufacturing Manual

## 1. Manufacturing objective

Create reusable, independently testable swarm blueprints that convert approved research, characters, ecosystems, lessons, code, media, and customer problems into quality-controlled products and business experiments.

The factory produces **proposals, artifacts, tests, and evidence**. It does not grant itself publication or financial authority.

## 2. Five Fable engines

### Fable Engine 1 — Reality and Evidence

Transforms approved sources, claims, uncertainty, contradictions, and corrections into a research-safe story foundation.

### Fable Engine 2 — Character and Ecosystem

Builds original characters, conflicts, environments, limitations, consent rules, and continuity maps.

### Fable Engine 3 — Learning and Build Mode

Turns the story into programming, blockchain, AI, cybersecurity, robotics, entrepreneurship, and evidence-literacy activities.

### Fable Engine 4 — Multimedia Adaptation

Plans ebook, audiobook, video-book, interactive-video, game, course, workbook, accessibility, and archival editions.

### Fable Engine 5 — Ethical Business and Franchise

Designs transparent offers, pricing tests, channels, affiliates, memberships, licenses, support, corrections, and certified franchise-node packages.

## 5. Ethical Revenue Machine state machine

`idea → customer_problem → evidence_needed → offer_draft → cost_model → rights_and_risk_review → Q3_experiment → human_approval → limited_launch → measure → correct_or_scale → retire_or_franchise`

A product may scale only when customer value, learner outcomes, support burden, refund patterns, costs, rights, privacy, accessibility, environmental impact, security, and revenue integrity are reviewed together.

## 6. Prohibited revenue behavior

- guaranteed income or funding claims;
- fabricated testimonials, scarcity, sales, credentials, or outcomes;
- unauthorized charges or subscriptions;
- hidden affiliate relationships;
- collecting private keys or seed phrases;
- misleading NFT investment claims;
- medical, legal, tax, financial, or investment conclusions without appropriate qualified review;
- automatic contracts or payments by an AI clone;
- selling an uncertified Q0–Q3 product as Q4 inventory.
