## 5. Quantum Questions Decision Engine

Every investigation, story, invention, agent, product, and business decision uses four questions.

### IF

- What must be true?
- What evidence should exist?
- What first-, second-, and third-order consequences follow?
- Who gains power and who carries risk?

### MAYBE

- What competing explanations remain?
- What uncertainty is being hidden?
- What evidence could change the conclusion?
- Could the same pattern arise from error, coincidence, corruption, drift, or misinterpretation?

### WHY NOT

- What constructive alternative can be built?
- Can the solution create safety, learning, accessibility, environmental repair, and lawful economic opportunity together?

### DON’T DO THAT

- Is the proposed action unsafe, deceptive, coercive, unlawful, exploitative, discriminatory, irreversible, or environmentally destructive?
- Does it depend on impersonation, fabricated certainty, hidden surveillance, private-data exposure, forced assimilation, or uncontrolled replication?

A failed DON’T-DO-THAT review stops work until an authorized human resolves the issue.

## 8. Privacy classes

### PUBLIC

Eligible for public repositories, public AI workspaces, products, and blockchain records after applicable gates.

### INTERNAL

Limited to approved business workspaces and private repositories.

### CONFIDENTIAL

Requires named authorization, purpose limitation, access logging, retention limits, and secure storage.

### RESTRICTED

Includes identity documents, medical records, financial account data, private evidence, witness identities, credentials, private keys, confidential legal material, and sensitive security information.

Restricted data must never enter public repositories, public AI workspaces, generalized creative prompts, NFT metadata, or public blockchains.
