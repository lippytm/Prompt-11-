### Required outputs

## Ethical Revenue Machine packet

### Objective

Design a bounded, transparent business experiment for a beginner Evidence Passport programming lab.

### Required fields

- customer or learner problem;
- target audience;
- proposed product and version;
- free-pilot scope;
- future price hypothesis, not a promise;
- expected costs and maximum budget;
- delivery channel;
- accessibility plan;
- support and refund plan;
- success, pause, and stop criteria;
- disclosures and prohibited claims;
- revenue, cost, learner outcome, complaint, and correction metrics;
- human approver.

### Prohibitions

No autonomous charging, account creation, contract acceptance, investment, borrowing, tax filing, asset transfer, guaranteed earnings, or misleading NFT claims.

## Shared prohibited data

Do not ingest or distribute passwords, API keys, private keys, seed phrases, full medical or financial records, confidential witnesses, private identity documents, or unredacted restricted evidence.
