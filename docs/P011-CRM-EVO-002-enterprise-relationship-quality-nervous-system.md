## Purpose

CRM-EVO turns the CRM and CQEM layers into a governed enterprise nervous system for every present and future Business of Businesses node. It connects relationship state, consent, service, learning, quality, provider status, innovation, correction, revenue-reference, environmental impact, and human decisions through stable IDs and evidence-backed events.

It does not create unrestricted autonomy. It compiles reviewable missions and sandbox-only evolution proposals for authorized humans to accept, reject, correct, roll back, or retire.

## Architecture

1. **Business Ecosystem Portfolio** — every business, product line, CRM, clone, agent, swarm, learning pathway, media system, provider adapter, and franchise node has a passport.
2. **Enterprise Relationship Graph** — typed edges describe governance, evidence, consent, routing, learning, support, provider, revenue, publishing, and correction relationships.
3. **Relationship Digital Twin** — maintains consent, service, learning, quality, risk, correction, and retirement state with provenance.
4. **Quality Telemetry Bus** — receives correlated events from CRM, MEPL, XPIO, EESI, CQEM, Innovation Forge, social publishing, support, learning, providers, and future systems.
5. **Fabric Hermes Mission Compiler** — maps signals to bounded swarms, required gates, privacy classes, blockers, budgets, timeouts, evidence, and HumanApproval state.
6. **Evolution Proposal Engine** — converts repeated gaps into deterministic sandbox-only proposals with tests, stop conditions, rollback, correction, and retirement.
7. **Provider Certification** — no connector is operational until purpose, minimum permissions, privacy ceiling, tests, logging, costs, failure behavior, reconciliation, shutdown, and revocation are verified.
8. **Outcome and Impact Intelligence** — measures service, learning, accessibility, fairness, environmental, correction, customer, community, and revenue-reference outcomes without hidden profiling.
9. **Fable 5 Learning Interface** — turns quality problems into stories, database labs, programming exercises, blockchain provenance projects, and entrepreneurship experiments.
10. **Universal Quality Contract** — scores prioritize review but never override a failed critical gate.

## Evolution cycle

`observe → correlate → validate → diagnose → compile mission → propose evolution → sandbox → test → red-team → compare independent model lines → human review → pilot → measure → correct, scale, roll back, or retire`

“Always evolving” means repeated evidence-backed and reversible cycles. It does not mean recursive self-modification, unlimited permissions, literal immortality, guaranteed growth, or permanent operation.

## Universal Quality Contract

Every Business of Businesses object must declare:

- stable ID, owner, purpose, stakeholders, lifecycle state, Q stage, RiskGate, and privacy ceiling;
- requirements, evidence, tests, defects, metrics, service/learning outcomes, accessibility, fairness, environmental methodology, and reliability;
- provider state, financial-authority boundary, critical gates, correction, rollback, revocation, retirement, and succession;
- HumanApproval state.

A score is never release authority. Q4 plus passed critical gates plus identified human approval are required for inventory eligibility.
