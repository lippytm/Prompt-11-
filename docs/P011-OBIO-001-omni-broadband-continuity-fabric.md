## 1. Purpose

The Omni-Broadband Input/Output Continuity and Learning Fabric, abbreviated **OBIO**, expands Prompt #11 into a device-neutral, platform-aware operating system for capturing, developing, reviewing, delivering, correcting, and continuing work across:

- iPhone;
- iPad;
- ASUS laptop;
- ChatGPT Business;
- GitHub repositories;
- lippytmai.zo.computer;
- Gemini and NotebookLM research lines;
- Claude and Fabric Hermes review/orchestration lines;
- Slack coordination;
- future approved platforms and devices.

“Broadband” means **greater multimodal, multi-device, multi-platform, multi-product input and output capacity**. It does not claim that Prompt #11 provides telecommunications service, unlimited network bandwidth, or automatic access to every listed platform.

OBIO exists to prevent valuable work from being lost, duplicated, silently contradicted, exposed to the wrong audience, or trapped inside one conversation, device, model, or application.

## 2. Mission

Convert ideas, evidence, questions, stories, code, research, lessons, business concepts, media, and user feedback into synchronized and quality-controlled work packets that can move safely through the Prompt #11 Systems of Systems.

The expected human outcomes are:

- better programmers;
- better blockchain and smart-contract developers;
- better AI and automation builders;
- better cybersecurity and privacy practitioners;
- better product designers and systems thinkers;
- better entrepreneurs and ethical business operators;
- better teachers, learners, reviewers, and community stewards.

## 3. Core laws

1. **One canonical identity per work item.** Every significant idea, task, product, source set, character, course, or business experiment receives a stable ID.
2. **One declared source of truth.** Public architecture and release state live in version-controlled GitHub records. Sensitive personal evidence remains in a separately controlled private vault.
3. **No silent synchronization.** Every transfer between devices or platforms produces a handoff or sync record.
4. **No repetition as verification.** Several AI systems repeating one source do not create independent corroboration.
5. **No platform may pass HumanApprovalGate.** Final release, minting, financial action, identity cloning, legal conclusion, or medical statement requires authorized human approval.
6. **No unrestricted connector.** Every platform receives only the minimum data and permissions required for its assigned work packet.
7. **No private secrets in public systems.** Credentials, keys, identity records, medical records, financial records, private witnesses, and restricted evidence remain outside public repositories, public Slack channels, NFT metadata, and public AI prompts.
8. **No proof, no inventory. No certification, no delivery. No human approval, no release.**
9. **Every output must be correctable.** Products require versioning, correction, supersession, revocation, retirement, and archive procedures.
10. **Every learning product must produce demonstrable practice.** Reading or watching alone does not qualify as mastery.

## 4. Authority hierarchy

When two platforms disagree, use this authority order:

1. signed or explicitly human-approved release record;
2. merged canonical GitHub `main` content and release manifest;
3. approved pull request or quality evidence packet;
4. open pull request and tracked GitHub issue;
5. registered Continuation Packet;
6. platform-specific working draft;
7. Slack status message;
8. unregistered chat, voice note, screenshot, or local draft.

A newer item does not automatically outrank an older approved record. It must identify what it supersedes and pass the applicable gates.

## 9. Continuation Packet

Every device or platform handoff uses a **Continuation Packet**.

Minimum fields:

- packet ID;
- project ID;
- parent module;
- workstream;
- current objective;
- origin device and platform;
- destination device or platform;
- model line;
- canonical repository, branch, issue, pull request, and commit references;
- completed work;
- work in progress;
- blocked work;
- exact next action;
- dependencies;
- risks;
- privacy classification;
- truth and fiction labels;
- current QA level and gate status;
- artifact manifest;
- last synchronized timestamp;
- human approval status;
- supersession reference.

A packet is a routing and continuity object. It is not proof that the work inside it is correct.

## 10. Work state machine

Each registered work item follows:

`captured → triaged → scoped → assigned → in_progress → review → changes_requested → quality_review → human_approval → approved → released → monitored → corrected_or_superseded → retired → archived`

Additional states:

- `blocked`;
- `quarantined`;
- `revoked`;
- `rejected`.

Every state transition records who or what changed the state, when it changed, the evidence supporting the change, and whether human approval was required.

## 11. Conflict resolution

When multiple devices or AI systems edit the same work:

1. preserve every version;
2. identify the last common approved ancestor;
3. produce a structured difference report;
4. classify differences as factual, creative, technical, legal, privacy, business, or formatting;
5. reconcile factual claims against sources rather than model preference;
6. retain valuable creative branches as named variants when appropriate;
7. send unresolved conflicts to a human reviewer;
8. record the decision and superseded versions;
9. run regression and continuity checks before release.

Never resolve a conflict by silently deleting the less convenient version.

## 13. Educational Entertainment pattern

Every major learning product should contain:

1. **Hook:** character, problem, mystery, or environmental conflict;
2. **Reality:** verified knowledge and explicit uncertainty;
3. **Story:** original fictional dramatization;
4. **Quantum Questions:** IF, MAYBE, WHY NOT, and DON’T DO THAT;
5. **Demonstration:** visible explanation or worked example;
6. **Build:** learner creates or repairs something;
7. **Test:** learner verifies behavior and documents defects;
8. **Business Application:** customer, stakeholder, or operating context;
9. **Reflection:** ethics, privacy, security, accessibility, and environmental effects;
10. **Proof of Learning:** repository, artifact, assessment, or reviewed demonstration;
11. **Next Mutation:** a harder or differently constrained version;
12. **Correction and Archive:** version, feedback, and supersession path.

## 14. Throughput without quality collapse

Greater broadband capacity requires controls against volume becoming noise.

Required controls:

- intake limits and triage queues;
- duplicate detection;
- canonical IDs;
- source manifests;
- model-line namespaces;
- work-in-progress limits;
- definition of ready;
- definition of done;
- automated tests where appropriate;
- human review for high-impact decisions;
- retention and retirement rules;
- cost and computational-credit budgets;
- learner and customer outcome measurement.

The factory must optimize **certified useful output**, not raw token count, file count, agent count, repository count, or NFT count.

## 16. Privacy classes

- **Public:** approved for public repositories, public media, and public NFT metadata.
- **Internal:** business operations and drafts for authorized collaborators.
- **Confidential:** limited project, customer, contract, or unpublished product information.
- **Restricted:** identity, health, financial, witness, credential, security, private-key, or legally sensitive information.

Restricted information must not be copied broadly among AI systems. Use redacted summaries and minimum-necessary disclosure.

## 17. Security baseline

Every platform and device should use, where supported:

- unique passwords;
- multifactor authentication;
- device passcodes and encryption;
- current software updates;
- account recovery methods;
- least-privilege permissions;
- secret managers rather than source-code secrets;
- session and device review;
- phishing-resistant authentication for critical accounts;
- encrypted backups;
- remote lock or wipe capability;
- incident and credential-rotation procedures.

No Continuation Packet may contain passwords, API keys, wallet seed phrases, private cryptographic keys, Social Security numbers, full medical records, or bank-account credentials.

## 18. Operational cadence

### Capture cycle

Use on any device whenever a new idea, source, decision, defect, or request appears.

### Daily continuity cycle

- review active packets;
- select the next highest-value work item;
- update blockers and risks;
- synchronize canonical links;
- create one exact next action;
- stop or quarantine unsafe work.

### Weekly quality cycle

- review WIP and duplicate work;
- review source freshness;
- review open defects and risks;
- review model-line independence;
- review learning and business metrics;
- review platform costs and credits;
- approve, correct, supersede, or retire work.

### Release cycle

- freeze candidate version;
- run tests and gates;
- assemble Quality Evidence Packet;
- obtain rights, privacy, accessibility, environmental, security, and business reviews;
- obtain HumanApprovalGate;
- release with manifest and correction channel;
- monitor outcomes.
