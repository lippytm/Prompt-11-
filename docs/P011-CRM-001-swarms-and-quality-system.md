# P-011-CRM-001 — CRM Swarms and Quality Assurance System

## Governing rule

> Quality and Quality Assurance is Job #1. No proof, no inventory. No certification, no delivery. No human approval, no release.

## Bounded swarm catalog

| Swarm | Primary responsibility | Principal block |
|---|---|---|
| Intake and Consent | validate purpose, source, channel, consent, lawful basis, suppression, and privacy | no consent or approved basis |
| Identity Resolution | normalize records and propose duplicate candidates | no automatic identity merge |
| Journey Orchestration | maintain evidence-backed lifecycle stage and next action | no hidden scoring or coercion |
| Lead and Opportunity | route transparent opportunities and offers | no AI financial authority |
| Learner Success | connect goals, skills, assessments, projects, mentors, and support | no guaranteed outcome |
| Customer Success | monitor delivery, value, accessibility, support, retention, and corrections | no manipulative retention |
| Partner and Affiliate | coordinate mentors, contributors, affiliates, partners, and franchise candidates | disclosure and rights required |
| Feedback and Research | collect approved feedback and aggregate patterns | no covert research or sensitive profiling |
| Attribution | connect sources, campaigns, products, interactions, and accounting references | estimated is not confirmed |
| Data Quality | measure completeness, validity, uniqueness, consistency, timeliness, and provenance | score cannot override gates |
| Privacy Rights | process access, correction, suppression, export, retention, and deletion requests | no AI denial of rights |
| Incident and Correction | contain, investigate, correct, notify, supersede, revoke, or retire | correction must propagate |
| QA Evidence | assemble requirements, tests, defects, risks, gate evidence, and QEPs | cannot pass HumanApprovalGate |

## Swarm Blueprint minimum fields

- swarm ID and version;
- human owner and reviewer;
- declared purpose;
- input and output schemas;
- privacy ceiling;
- approved source systems and destinations;
- permitted tools and prohibited actions;
- budget and timeout;
- test suite and evaluation thresholds;
- model-line identity;
- audit correlation and evidence references;
- stop-work conditions;
- rollback, shutdown, revocation, correction, and retirement;
- HumanApprovalGate requirements.

## CRM event-to-mission flow

1. Receive a consent-safe event.
2. Validate party, purpose, source, privacy, and suppression.
3. Create a stable correlation ID.
4. Classify the event without making a high-impact decision.
5. Propose a bounded mission.
6. Attach required gates and human review.
7. Route minimum-necessary data through Fabric Hermes.
8. Record result, evidence, defects, cost, and next action.
9. Correct or retire the mission when conditions change.

Example mission families:

- learner support;
- onboarding accessibility;
- lead routing;
- service handoff;
- retention follow-up;
- partner review;
- consent or freshness review;
- complaint and correction;
- attribution reconciliation.

## Release evaluation

Release eligibility requires all of the following:

- Q4 certification;
- no Red RiskGate;
- no failed or conditional release-blocking critical gates;
- current consent, privacy, security, accessibility, fairness, service, attribution, retention, correction, and financial-authority evidence;
- HumanApprovalGate approved by an identified authorized human;
- approved product, audience, channel, message, and provider-adapter scope;
- monitoring and correction channel.

An automated score, model agreement, CRM stage, or revenue result cannot override these conditions.

## Test categories

- deterministic stable IDs and hashes;
- source and provenance requirements;
- consent ordering and withdrawal;
- suppression and communication blocking;
- identity duplicate detection without merging;
- prohibited sensitive purpose rejection;
- mission routing and mandatory human review;
- support SLA and escalation;
- financial and refund authority;
- confirmed attribution and accounting reference;
- release blocking and HumanApprovalGate;
- database integrity, backup, restore, and migration;
- adapter idempotency, conflict, failure, rollback, shutdown, and revocation;
- correction propagation;
- accessibility and fairness review.

## Continuous improvement

CRM improvements follow:

`observe → measure → identify gap → propose mutation → sandbox → test → red-team → compare independent model lines → human review → approve or reject → version → monitor → correct, roll back, or retire`

No swarm or clone may activate its own mutation or broaden its own access.
