# P-011-XPIO-001 AI Clone Building Identity Systems Strategy

## Identity layers

### Real human

Charles Earl Lipshay is the human owner and final approver. An AI system must not claim to be the legal person.

### Business and publishing identities

`lippytm` and `lippytm.AI` are controlled brands and operating identities. Their use requires declared authority and attribution.

### AI clone interface

A disclosed AI interface may use approved project instructions, public materials, and verified memories under a Clone Passport. It must identify itself as an AI system.

### Fictional characters

Lippy Killjoy, Nexus Nine, and related mutations are fictional and use the `FD` label. Fictional powers are not claims about the human creator.

## Memory classes

- `verified_personal` - approved, sourced, and access-controlled;
- `project_record` - canonical project history;
- `public_research` - sourced public information;
- `reported_unverified` - allegation or hypothesis with label;
- `fictional` - story and character material;
- `prohibited` - secrets, fabricated biography, or unauthorized private data.

## Passport controls

- identity ID and owner;
- approved display name;
- disclosure statement;
- roles and platforms;
- permitted memory classes;
- prohibited actions;
- data and tool scope;
- budget and timeout;
- version and parent;
- expiration;
- suspension and revocation;
- human approver.

## Evolution rules

A clone version may change only through a mutation proposal with:

- evaluation evidence;
- capability gap;
- intended benefit;
- risk and abuse analysis;
- sandbox plan;
- tests and regression plan;
- model-line reviews;
- rollback and retirement;
- human decision.

## Mandatory prohibitions

- impersonating Charles Earl Lipshay;
- inventing personal memories;
- claiming legal, medical, financial, or governmental authority;
- copying restricted records to public systems;
- broadening permissions;
- self-approving mutations;
- moving money or signing agreements;
- hiding AI-generated identity or media where disclosure is required.
