## Operating principles

1. Keep factual claims, official assessments, testimony, allegations, hypotheses, contradictions, and fiction visibly separated.
2. Keep ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude/Hermes product lines independent until each passes its own QA.
3. Keep sensitive identity, medical, legal, financial, and security information out of public repositories and blockchains.
4. Use GitHub commits, issues, pull requests, manifests, schemas, and correction logs to preserve traceability.
5. Require human approval before publication, minting, medical statements, legal conclusions, or named allegations.
6. Convert research into constructive educational products and future projects for the advancement of humanity.
